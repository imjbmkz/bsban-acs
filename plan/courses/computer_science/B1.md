**Resources:** Kleinberg & Tardos, Algorithm Design

## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Kleinberg & Tardos problems, 2-hour timed set
- **Build (30%):** Sketching library (MinHash, HyperLogLog, Count-Min) with error analysis — in `work/B1/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Approximate answers at a fraction of the cost: when to use them — in `work/B1/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B1` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Greedy algorithms and exchange arguments | Interval scheduling with proofs |
| 2 | – | Divide and conquer, recurrences | Closest pair and counting inversions |
| 3 | – | Dynamic programming, advanced | Sequence alignment for fuzzy record matching |
| 4 | – | Network flow | Bipartite matching for resource allocation |
| 5 | – | NP-completeness and reductions | Reduction proofs |
| 6 | – | Approximation algorithms | Approximate set cover for sensor placement |
| 7 | – | Randomized algorithms | Reservoir sampling and MinHash |
| 8 | – | Streaming and sketching algorithms | HyperLogLog and Count-Min sketch from scratch |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/B1/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/B1/`.
