# Assessment rubrics

For courses without a public exam. Score each criterion, add up, and pass at 70%. Where possible,
ask a colleague or peer to score you; self-scoring is a fallback, and be strict.

## Practical exam

60 minutes, from a clean clone, no notes beyond official docs. Used for C102.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Seeded bug found and fixed, with a test that would have caught it | 30 |
| CI is green: lint, types and tests | 30 |
| Commit history and PR description explain the change | 20 |
| Finished within 60 minutes | 20 |

## Timed case

60–90 minutes: a problem statement or dataset you have not seen (from a case book, a Kaggle
dataset, or a colleague), ending in a one-page recommendation. Used for B103, B203, B302, B5, C4.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Problem framed correctly; issue tree is MECE | 20 |
| Analysis uses the course's methods correctly | 30 |
| Recommendation leads, is specific and quantified | 25 |
| Risks and next step stated | 15 |
| Within time and one page | 10 |

## System design interview

60 minutes, recorded, with a peer asking follow-ups if possible. Used for C302, B3.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Requirements and scale clarified before designing | 15 |
| Data, training, serving and monitoring covered end to end | 35 |
| Trade-offs named with numbers (latency, cost, freshness) | 25 |
| Failure modes and how they are detected | 15 |
| Clear diagram and communication | 10 |

## Textbook timed set

Pick 6–8 end-of-chapter problems you have not seen, spread across the course's units, with
solutions available. 2 hours, closed book. Score each problem out of 10 against the solution.

## Pitch

15 minutes plus questions, to someone who could plausibly buy. Used for B401.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Customer and problem are specific and evidenced | 25 |
| Offer and pricing are clear, with unit economics | 25 |
| Why you, why now | 15 |
| Answers to questions | 20 |
| Within time | 15 |

## Negotiation role-play

Two role-plays with a partner using a published case, scored by the partner. Used for C5.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation: BATNA, reservation point, interests | 30 |
| Created value through trade-offs across issues | 30 |
| Claimed a fair share of value | 20 |
| Relationship kept intact | 20 |

## Capstone

Used for X401–X402; scored by the client and one technical reviewer.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The client made a measurable decision from the work | 35 |
| Repo reproduces end to end with tests | 25 |
| Report and presentation are clear for both audiences | 20 |
| Client testimonial you can cite | 20 |
