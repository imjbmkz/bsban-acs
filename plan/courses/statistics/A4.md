## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `A4` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

Caution: this is part of the math and statistics core that master's admissions check first. The guide recommends taking it in full; its timed final is your proof.

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Returns, stylized facts of financial data | Analyze PSEi or global index returns |
| 2 | – | ARMA models for returns | Fit and test ARMA on returns |
| 3 | – | Volatility: ARCH and GARCH | GARCH volatility forecast |
| 4 | – | Value at risk and expected shortfall | Historical, parametric and Monte Carlo VaR |
| 5 | – | Backtesting risk models | Kupiec test on your VaR |
| 6 | – | Multivariate models and correlation | Portfolio covariance estimation |
| 7 | – | Credit risk basics | Probability-of-default scorecard |
| 8 | – | Stress testing and model risk | Stress scenario report |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/A4/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/A4/`.
