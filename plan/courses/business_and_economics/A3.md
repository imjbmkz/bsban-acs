- **Build (30%):** Panel or IV study on Philippine regional data — in `work/A3/build/`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `A3` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Econometric thinking and OLS assumptions | Replicate a textbook wage regression |
| 2 | – | Inference and asymptotics in OLS | Robust inference on that model |
| 3 | – | Specification, functional form, proxies | Specification tests |
| 4 | – | Heteroskedasticity and serial correlation | Diagnose and correct both |
| 5 | – | Panel data: fixed and random effects | Panel model on regional data |
| 6 | – | Instrumental variables and 2SLS | 2SLS estimate with weak-instrument checks |
| 7 | – | Limited dependent variables: probit, logit, Tobit | Probit model of a binary decision |
| 8 | – | Time-series regression basics | Distributed lag model of advertising |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/A3/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/A3/`.
