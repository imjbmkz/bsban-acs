## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B2` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

Caution: this is part of the math and statistics core that master's admissions check first. The guide recommends taking it in full; its timed final is your proof.

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Optimization for statistics: Newton, Fisher scoring | Fit a GLM by IRLS from scratch |
| 2 | – | The EM algorithm | Gaussian mixture by EM |
| 3 | – | Simulation and random number generation | Inverse-transform and rejection sampling |
| 4 | – | Monte Carlo integration and variance reduction | Importance sampling for a rare-event probability |
| 5 | – | MCMC: Gibbs and Metropolis-Hastings | Gibbs sampler for a hierarchical model |
| 6 | – | Bootstrap methods | BCa intervals |
| 7 | – | Density estimation and smoothing | Kernel density estimation with bandwidth selection |
| 8 | – | Scaling computation: vectorization, JAX or Numba | Speed up a sampler 10x |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/B2/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/B2/`.
