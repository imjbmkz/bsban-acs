**Resources:** Minto, The Pyramid Principle; Conn & McLean, Bulletproof Problem Solving

## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed case: 60 minutes to structure a provided problem and write an executive summary
- **Build (30%):** Consulting deck that retells one past work project as a recommendation — in `work/B103/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Executive summary that gets a decision — in `work/B103/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B103` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
