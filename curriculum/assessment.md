# Assessment

Grade like a university would: a course counts as passed only when all three parts are done and the
timed exam clears 70%.

| Component | Weight | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exam | 50% | A timed, closed-book final from the course's free materials (MIT OCW and Stat 110 publish past exams with solutions) |
| Build | 30% | A public repo or folder with tests, a README and reproducible results |
| Business memo | 20% | One page, reviewed by a colleague or manager who would actually make that decision |

**Transcript.** Keep one tracker of course, dates, exam score and repo link
([`progress/transcript.csv`](../progress/transcript.csv) once the weekly plan is merged). It becomes
the backbone of the statement of purpose.

## First 30 days

- [ ] Take a free placement check (Khan Academy precalculus and calculus unit tests) to see if M101 can be skipped.
- [ ] Start M101 (or M102) and C101/C102 in parallel.
- [ ] Set up the course tracker and the 40/40/20 templates.
- [ ] Shortlist 5 target master's programs and note their prerequisites.
