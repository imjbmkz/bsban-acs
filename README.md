# BS Business Analytics — Analytical Consulting & Solutions

A self-designed, degree-style study program in statistics, computer science and business analytics,
built as preparation for one of three master's directions:

- **MS Statistics** with a finance, industrial engineering or economics minor
- **MS Computer Science** with a statistical analysis specialization
- **MBA** with an analytics concentration

The program is 40 courses over 4 levels, about 10 hours a week, with every course split
40% theory, 40% practice, 20% business application.
