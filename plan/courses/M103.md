## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 6 | [36](../weeks/year-1.md#week-36) | Inclusion-exclusion, binomial theorem, recurrence relations | Solve a recurrence for a work backlog that grows and drains each week |

| 8 | [39](../weeks/year-1.md#week-39) | Topological sort, shortest paths, matchings | Implement topological sort and Dijkstra; schedule jobs by dependency |
| 9 | [40](../weeks/year-1.md#week-40) | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |
