## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** OpenIntro end-of-chapter exercises as a 2-hour timed practice exam
- **Build (30%):** EDA and inference report on a public dataset, reproducible from a clean clone — in `work/S101/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Is the sales uplift real or noise? A recommendation to a sales manager — in `work/S101/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `S101` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [21](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-21) | Data, study design, observational vs experimental, sampling bias | Critique the sampling behind one dataset you use at work |
| 2 | [22](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-22) | Numerical and categorical summaries, distributions, visualization | EDA notebook on a public dataset (e.g., PSA OpenSTAT or a Kaggle retail dataset) |
| 3 | [23](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-23) | Probability basics and conditional probability | Compute conversion probabilities by customer segment from data |
| 4 | [25](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-25) | Normal and binomial distributions | Simulate both in NumPy and compare against the formulas |
| 5 | [26](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-26) | Sampling distributions, central limit theorem, confidence intervals | Bootstrap vs formula confidence interval for average order value |
| 6 | [27](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-27) | Hypothesis testing, p-values, type I and II errors | Test whether a sales uplift after a campaign is significant |
| 7 | [28](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-28) | Inference for proportions and means: t-tests, chi-square | Two-sample t-test and chi-square test on segment data |
| 8 | [29](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-29) | Simple and multiple linear regression | Regress sales on price and promotion; interpret each coefficient in plain words |
| 9 | [30](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-30) | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/S101/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/S101/`.
