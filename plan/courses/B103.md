## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed case: 60 minutes to structure a provided problem and write an executive summary
- **Build (30%):** Consulting deck that retells one past work project as a recommendation — in `work/B103/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Executive summary that gets a decision — in `work/B103/memo.md`
