## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed HBR case analysis (90 minutes)
- **Build (30%):** Leadership portfolio: assessments, plans and reflections — in `work/C4/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Change management plan for adopting analytics in a team — in `work/C4/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `C4` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).
