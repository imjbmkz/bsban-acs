## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `C3` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Process analysis: flow rate, utilization, Little's law | Analyze a real process with Little's law |
| 2 | – | Bottlenecks and capacity | Find a bottleneck in a pipeline or operation |
| 3 | – | Variability and waiting time | Queue simulation of a service desk |
| 4 | – | Inventory: EOQ and the newsvendor model | Newsvendor order quantity from forecast error |
| 5 | – | Service levels and safety stock | Safety stock policy from S304 forecasts |
| 6 | – | Supply chain coordination and the bullwhip effect | Beer game simulation |
| 7 | – | Lean operations | Value-stream map of a process |
| 8 | – | Operations strategy | Operations improvement plan |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/C3/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/C3/`.
