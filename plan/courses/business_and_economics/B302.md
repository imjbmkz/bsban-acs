## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed case interview with a peer (45 minutes, recorded)
- **Build (30%):** Market-sizing model plus a log of case-interview drills — in `work/B302/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Strategy recommendation for a real company — in `work/B302/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B302` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).
