## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed negotiation role-play scored by a partner
- **Build (30%):** Negotiation playbook with prepared plans and role-play notes — in `work/C5/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Corporate strategy recommendation for a real company — in `work/C5/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `C5` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Corporate strategy: diversification and portfolio | Portfolio analysis of a conglomerate |
| 2 | – | Mergers, acquisitions and alliances | Deal rationale write-up for a recent deal |
| 3 | – | Platform and data strategy | Data moat analysis of a company |
| 4 | – | Negotiation basics: interests, BATNA, ZOPA | Prepare a negotiation plan |
| 5 | – | Distributive negotiation | Role-play a price negotiation |
| 6 | – | Integrative negotiation and value creation | Role-play a multi-issue deal |
| 7 | – | Negotiating contracts and scope for services | Draft a statement of work for a consulting deal |
| 8 | – | Negotiating salary and relocation packages | Negotiation plan for a job offer abroad |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/C5/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/C5/`.
