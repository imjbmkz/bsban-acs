# Program overview

**BS Business Analytics, major in Analytical Consulting & Solutions (self-designed)**

A 4-level, 40-course self-study "degree" that gives a working data engineer the math, statistics,
computer science and business foundation a BSBA did not, so any of three master's paths is open
afterward. It is built for a part-time learner: about 10 hours a week, finishing in just under 4 years
(see [`plan/`](../plan/) for the week-by-week schedule).

## The three target directions

| Target master's | What admissions will check | What this curriculum supplies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MS Statistics + finance / IE / economics minor | Calculus I–III, linear algebra, probability, mathematical statistics, some programming | Full math and theory track (Levels 1–3) plus a domain elective cluster |
| MS Computer Science + statistical analysis | Discrete math, data structures, algorithms, systems, programming depth | Full CS track, with stats theory as the specialization |
| MBA, analytics concentration | Work experience, GMAT/GRE quant, business literacy | Business track, consulting capstone, and quantitative fluency that sets you apart |

## Design principles

- **Theory, practice, business in every course.** Each course ends with a proof or derivation set,
  a coded build, and a business memo or case.
- **Reuse the day job.** Six years in pipelines, SQL and Databricks count; those topics are compressed
  (marked *test-out*) so time goes to math and statistics, the real gaps.
- **Evidence over effort.** Master's committees read transcripts, not self-study. Every level names a
  credential or exit check that proves it (see [paths and admissions](paths-and-admissions.md)).

**Assumptions:** strong SQL and Python, production data engineering, high-school-level math that may
be rusty, no formal calculus or proof writing.
