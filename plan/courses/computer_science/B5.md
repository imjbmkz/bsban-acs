## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Timed scenario: 90 minutes to assess a dataset's compliance risks
- **Build (30%):** Governance pack: classification, access model, privacy checks and threat model — in `work/B5/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Privacy compliance plan for a client moving data abroad — in `work/B5/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B5` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | – | Data governance frameworks and roles | Governance RACI for a data platform |
| 2 | – | Privacy law: Philippine DPA, GDPR, Australian Privacy Act | Compare obligations across the three |
| 3 | – | Access control and data classification | Classification and access model in Unity Catalog |
| 4 | – | Encryption, key management, secrets | Secrets handling review of a pipeline |
| 5 | – | Anonymization and pseudonymization | k-anonymity check on a dataset |
| 6 | – | Differential privacy | Differentially private counts with a privacy budget |
| 7 | – | Data lineage, auditing and quality ownership | Lineage and audit report for one pipeline |
| 8 | – | Security threats to data platforms | Threat model of your portfolio stack |
| 9 | – | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/B5/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/B5/`.
