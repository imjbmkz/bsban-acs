## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Pitch to a reviewer who scores it against a rubric (timed, 15 minutes)
- **Build (30%):** Business plan and pricing model for an analytics consulting service — in `work/B401/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Your own consulting offer, priced — in `work/B401/memo.md`

Confident in this material? Add `B401` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).
