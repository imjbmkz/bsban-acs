**Resources:** Hillier & Lieberman, Introduction to Operations Research; SimPy and OR-Tools docs

## Final deliverables

- **Exam (50%, pass at 70%):** Hillier & Lieberman problems, 2-hour timed set
- **Build (30%):** Scheduling or routing optimizer with a simple front end — in `work/B301/build/`
- **Business memo (20%):** Warehouse or workforce optimization case — in `work/B301/memo.md`

## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `B301` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).
