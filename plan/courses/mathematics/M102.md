## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [10](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-10) | Limits and continuity | Estimate limits numerically in Python; find where a tiered pricing function is discontinuous |
| 2 | [13](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-13) | Derivative definition; power, product and quotient rules | Implement a finite-difference derivative and compare it with analytic results |
| 3 | [14](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-14) | Chain rule, implicit differentiation, derivatives of exp and log | Derive and code marginal revenue for a fitted demand curve |
| 4 | [15](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-15) | Related rates and linear approximation | Approximate the revenue effect of a 1% price change and check it against the exact value |
| 5 | [16](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-16) | Optimization, extrema, curve sketching | Find the profit-maximizing price for a fitted demand and cost curve |
| 6 | [17](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-17) | Mean value theorem, L'Hopital's rule, Newton's method | Implement Newton's method to solve break-even and rate-of-return equations |
| 7 | [18](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-18) | Riemann sums and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus | Code trapezoid and Riemann integrators; total revenue from a marginal revenue curve |
| 8 | [19](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-19) | Substitution, area between curves, average value | Compute consumer surplus from a demand curve and average daily load from a usage curve |
| 9 | [20](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-20) | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/M102/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/M102/`.
