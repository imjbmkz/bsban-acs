# Level 4 — Specialization & Capstone

Level 4 has 3 common courses, 5 electives from the track matching the chosen master's, and a
2-course consulting capstone.

## Common courses (all tracks)

| Code | Course | Prereq | Theory | Practice build | Business application | Main resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S401 | Causal Inference & Experimental Design | S302 | Potential outcomes, DAGs, diff-in-diff, IV, RDD | Re-analyze a policy or pricing change causally | "Did our initiative actually work?" | Cunningham *Causal Inference: The Mixtape*; Huntington-Klein *The Effect* (both free) |
| S402 | Bayesian Statistics | S301 | Priors, posteriors, hierarchical models, MCMC | Hierarchical model in PyMC | Decisions under uncertainty with small data | McElreath *Statistical Rethinking* (free lectures) |
| B401 | Analytics Ventures: Product, Pricing & Entrepreneurship | B302 | Product strategy, pricing models, unit economics | Business plan + pricing model for an analytics service | Your own consulting offer, priced | Ries *The Lean Startup*; Ramanujam *Monetizing Innovation* |

## Track electives — pick one track, take all 5

| Track A: Statistics + domain | Track B: CS + statistics | Track C: MBA in analytics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 Real Analysis I (required) | B1 Advanced Algorithm Design | C1 Managerial Economics & Pricing |
| A2 Stochastic Processes (required) | B2 Computational Statistics (bootstrap, MCMC, EM) | C2 Valuation & Financial Modeling |
| A3 Econometrics *(economics minor)* | B3 Machine Learning Engineering at Scale | C3 Operations & Supply Chain Management |
| A4 Financial Econometrics & Risk *(finance minor)* | B4 Distributed Systems Design | C4 Leadership & Organizational Behavior |
| A5 Quality Engineering, SPC & DOE *(industrial engineering minor)* | B5 Data Privacy, Security & Governance | C5 Corporate Strategy & Negotiation |

Track A: take A1, A2, then three courses in the minor area, swapping in domain titles where needed
(e.g., A3–A5 all become finance courses for a finance minor). Real Analysis matters most here: many
statistics programs expect or strongly prefer it.

## Capstone (X401–X402): a real consulting engagement

1. **X401 Scoping & research (≈60 h):** find a real client (a small business, NGO, or employer),
   write an issue tree, a data audit and a statement of work.
2. **X402 Delivery (≈90 h):** build the pipeline, the statistical model and a decision tool; deliver
   a board-style presentation and a technical report.
3. **Graded on:** a measurable decision the client made, the reproducibility of the repo, and a
   client testimonial to cite in applications.
