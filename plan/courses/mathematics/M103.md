## Fast track

Confident in this material? Add `M103` to `fast_track` in plan/config.yaml. The course then takes 3 weeks instead of 9: a timed diagnostic using the assessment above, the build, then the memo. Below 70% on the diagnostic, switch to the full course. See [the fast-track guide](../../fast-track.md).

Caution: this is part of the math and statistics core that master's admissions check first. The guide recommends taking it in full; its timed final is your proof.

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [31](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-31) | Propositional logic, truth tables, quantifiers | Express SQL WHERE clauses as logical statements and test equivalences in Python |
| 2 | [32](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-32) | Sets, functions, relations | Model joins and set operations on sample tables; verify results in SQL |
| 3 | [33](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-33) | Direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction | Write five short proofs and typeset them in Markdown with LaTeX |
| 4 | [34](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-34) | Induction and strong induction | Prove a loop invariant for two of your own pipeline functions |
| 5 | [35](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-35) | Counting: permutations, combinations, pigeonhole principle | Count partitioning and configuration options for a table design |
| 6 | [36](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-36) | Inclusion-exclusion, binomial theorem, recurrence relations | Solve a recurrence for a work backlog that grows and drains each week |
| 7 | [38](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-38) | Graphs: paths, trees, connectivity, DAGs | Build your data lineage as a networkx DAG and detect cycles |
| 8 | [39](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-39) | Topological sort, shortest paths, matchings | Implement topological sort and Dijkstra; schedule jobs by dependency |
| 9 | [40](../../weeks/year-1.md#week-40) | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/M103/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/M103/`.
