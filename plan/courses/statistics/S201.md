Caution: this is part of the math and statistics core that master's admissions check first. The guide recommends taking it in full; its timed final is your proof.

**Milestone at the end:** Level 2 exit check, part 2: Stat 110 final at 70% or better.

## Week by week

| Week | Plan week | Learn | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [60](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-60) | Counting and naive probability (book ch. 1) | Simulate the birthday problem and check it against the formula |
| 2 | [61](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-61) | Conditional probability and Bayes' rule (ch. 2) | Base-rate calculator for a fraud or anomaly alert |
| 3 | [62](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-62) | Discrete random variables: Bernoulli, binomial, hypergeometric, geometric (ch. 3-4) | Model defects per batch; simulate vs PMF |
| 4 | [65](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-65) | Expectation, linearity, indicator variables, Poisson (ch. 4) | Expected support tickets per hour with a Poisson model; staffing implications |
| 5 | [66](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-66) | Continuous random variables: uniform, normal, exponential (ch. 5) | Fit time-between-events from your pipeline logs to an exponential |
| 6 | [67](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-67) | Moments and moment generating functions (ch. 6) | Mean, variance and skew of an insurance claim distribution |
| 7 | [68](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-68) | Joint distributions, covariance, correlation (ch. 7) | Joint model of order size and order frequency |
| 8 | [69](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-69) | Conditional expectation, inequalities, LLN and CLT (ch. 9-10) | Monte Carlo warranty-cost simulator with confidence bands |
| 9 | [70](../../weeks/year-2.md#week-70) | **Final week:** timed exam | Finish the build and the business memo |

Each study week: work the reading or lectures, then the problems, and keep one page of notes in `work/S201/notes/week-NN.md`. Commit the apply task to `work/S201/`.
